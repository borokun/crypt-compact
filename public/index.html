      How to play

      Controls

      Phone and keyboard

      Move
      Drag anywhere with your thumb: Wren moves as far as your thumb does. Prefer a thumbstick? Switch it in the pause menu.
      Shield and focus
      Hold Ward. The shield aims at Dracula, and Wren slows to 45% with his hitbox ringed, for threading tight gaps.

      Pause

      Moves

      Six ways to fight back

      GrazeLet a purple shot brush past without touching its dark core. Each graze fills the lantern a little.
      LanternA switch (L). Off, Wren's light shrinks, faster when standing still, and purple shots outside it are invisible (window beams and snipes always show). On, it reveals everything and burns oil, but Dracula attacks 25% faster with 25% faster shots. Oil refills as you move and reflect. Run dry and Wren slows by 30% for three seconds.
      FlareGrazing purple shots charges the flare. Light the lantern with a full charge and it flares: a ring of light turns every purple shot it touches red.
      RollA quick tumble (Shift) that no shot can touch. Roll through a shot at the last instant for a night step: the room slows for a second and every block counts as perfect.
      Ward meterThe shield drains 15% a second while held and never refills on its own. Each perfect block on a red shot adds 20%. Run it dry and Ward locks for 1.5 seconds, then reforms at 20%.
      TripleLand three perfect blocks in a row and the third shot splits into three gold shots. Three pips over Wren count the chain.
      GarlicCrushing a bulb (G) roots Wren for 0.4 seconds: no moving, no shield, no roll, and any hit does double damage. Finish it and a 1.5-second cloud dissolves red shots. Every fifth one leaves a heart shard that heals 10, up to three per cloud. Four shards make a heart. Three bulbs per fight.
      Pillar kickRoll into a standing pillar and it topples into a low wall that stops everything, beams included, for eight seconds.

      Bullet language

      Color tells you what to do

      BloodRed shots. The shield reflects them. This is your ammunition.
      ShadowViolet shots, including Dracula's anti-camping snipes. They pass through the shield.
      ReflectedFlies along your aim and deals 3 damage.
      PerfectShield raised within 0.153 s of impact. Homes in for 8 damage and refunds stamina and oil.
      Open windowDracula blocks reflected shots unless his guard is open: right after he finishes a pattern. Shots that land then deal double damage.

      Core loop

      Read, wait, flick

      1. Read the pattern: which shots are red and which are violet.
      2. Slip between the violet ones. The hitbox is the white dot at Wren's belt, not the whole cloak.
      3. Let the red ones come close, then raise the shield at the last moment for a gold parry. Save your best reflections for when Dracula's guard is open.
      4. Hide behind the stone pillars when it gets dense. They soak up 25 shots each, then crumble.
      5. Stay off the glowing green cracks. They halve your speed.
      6. Drop the shield to let stamina refill. A cracked shield locks you out for a second.
      7. Keep moving. Stay in one small area for about two seconds and a violet ring marks the spot. Dracula then fires aimed purple shots at it until you leave.
